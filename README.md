One voicemail greeting recorded by Mittens, a cat far too busy knocking things off the counter to take your call — and honest enough to admit she will ignore your message at 4 a.m. anyway. It is a cat's voicemail through and through: the greeting format, the shelf-clearing mischief, and the 4 a.m. attitude, landing its punch in a single spoken breath.

Run it: python3 main.py
